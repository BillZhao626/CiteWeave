Wire v6 clarifies the existing topic contract. Choose the intended stable task and supported subject before the relation enum. An exact old History task plus its supported entity after intervening different head activity is return. Continue is the same head task, not merely continuation of any earlier discussion. Shift cannot have dependency required or inherited origins; it is a genuinely self-contained new intent. The same entity may occur in a new task as an exact current literal; an older State item's age alone does not prove return. Do not invent an old task when only State is available. If an inherited State-only intent is being resumed, explicitly choose return; a new action phrase appended to a task label does not by itself establish a new independent topic. Valid singleton references to exact active State are allowed for ordinary State-only intent and retain their binding; unsupported/multiple candidates and missing correction groups still fail closed. Neither the normal form nor this guidance supplies documentary facts or authorizes hidden raw History recovery.
